Context: This is a timer that simulates tea steeping. As the timer runs out, the tea gets darker.

  • Context: I created this timer based off of the concept of waiting for your tea to steep. This timer is meant to be used for that, helping you visualize how your tea is steeping while you leave it in the pot. However, it could be used for other purposes, as long as it only requires minutes and seconds.
  • Design decisions: I intentionally made the timer only accept shorter inputs (minutes and seconds) asa tea does not ususally need to steep for longer than that.
  • Future work: I think if I had more knowledge this could include instructions or suggestions for how long the tea should steep.

Sketch narrative

Context: Apple watch face based off of the apple fitness rings.

  • Context: I made this clock because I personally found the Apple Watch's face to be very confusing and difficult to read. I based this design off of a layout I did enjoy looking at, my fitness rings.
  • Design decisions: I chose to base the colors off of the rings, I made them a bit more muted so they would be easier to look at on a big screen. I aslo chose to display the military time in the middle so users would have a clear idea of the exact time. As for the date, I originally had a DD/MM/YYYY layout, but I decided to remove the year information to make more space for putting the name of the day and month abbreviation, similar to the time summary you get on iPhones.
  • Future work: I could include some sort of event marks on the day ring, so you know if you're getting close to an event later. I could also add some sort of customization feature ofr the colors of the ring.

Design Notes:

  • Why this idea? I wanted to visualize my monthly trends for spotufy listening and see if there were any trends throughout the months.
  • Why this title? I wanted to make a pun with the seasons and spotify listening.
  • Is this Author driven or user driven? What Genre of visualization is this? I would consider this to be an author driven visualization, as there are not many interactive components for users to use.
  • What story is being communicated? I tend to listento less music on spotify in Autumn, as October, November, and December were consistently below my overall average listening minutes for the year. Given the amount of information being communicated, I tried to make the visuals as simplisitc and clear as possible, and added labels for each datapoint so people could easily tell what the labels were meant to communicate. I also tried to limit the amount of colors that were present, and only colored in information that was important, like seasons, overall average, and the data points themselves.
