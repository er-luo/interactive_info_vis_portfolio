Sketch narrative

Context: This is a timer that simulates tea steeping. As the timer runs out, the tea gets darker.

  • Context: I created this timer based off of the concept of waiting for your tea to steep. This timer is meant to be used for that, helping you visualize how your tea is steeping while you leave it in the pot. However, it could be used for other purposes, as long as it only requires minutes and seconds.
  • Design decisions: I intentionally made the timer only accept shorter inputs (minutes and seconds) asa tea does not ususally need to steep for longer than that.
  • Future work: I think if I had more knowledge this could include instructions or suggestions for how long the tea should steep.
